How it works

From scattered files
to a claim-ready report

Say the word. claim-ready does the rest, using the files already on your NAS.

01

Discover your assets

Scans your authorized NAS folders for photos of physical items, invoices, receipts, and warranty PDFs. Groups results by your existing folder structure.

⚡ Files capability
02

Organize by room & category

Groups photos by room (living room, kitchen, garage…) and item type (electronics, appliances, furniture, tools). Inferred from album metadata or folder names.

⚡ Albums capability
03

Extract structured data

Reads each invoice and warranty document to extract brand, model, serial number, purchase date, price, and retailer. Marks missing fields clearly — never invents values.

⚡ Documents capability
04

Generate the report

Compiles everything into a structured Markdown report organized by category, with a summary of documentation gaps your adjuster will need to know about.

📄 Saved to your NAS
05

Your confirmation, always

Nothing is written without your explicit OK. claim-ready shows you what it found and asks before saving anything.

Privacy by design

Your inventory stays yours

A home inventory is a list of what you own and what it's worth. That data doesn't belong in the cloud.

☁️ Cloud apps (Encircle, etc.)
✗Your photos and valuations stored on third-party servers
✗Privacy policy can change after you upload
✗Breach risk: your belongings list exposed
✗Subscription required for full features
✗Data can be subpoenaed or shared with insurers
🏠 claim-ready on UGREEN NAS
✓All processing on your local hardware
✓No file ever leaves your NAS
✓You own the output — a plain Markdown file
✓Works during internet outages and after a disaster
✓Uses files you already have — no extra app to feed
